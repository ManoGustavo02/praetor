[h2]1.5.0[/h2]
[b]English[/b]
The whole interface was redone, and DarkRP servers get their own HUD and F4 menu.
[list]
[*][b]New design[/b] for the menu and every window: new fonts (Bagel Fat One and Nunito, SIL Open Font License), purple as the main colour, all 116 icons redrawn and new interface sounds (CC0), with a volume setting in your options.
[*][b]Chat[/b] redrawn. Fixed: holding the click inside the chat made the text box lose and get back the focus ("Message to everyone" blinking).
[*][b]Scoreboard (TAB)[/b] redrawn, with a [b]DarkRP version[/b]: players grouped by job with the job colour and free slots, salary, wanted (blinking), arrested and gun license, police and wanted counters. Optional music that fades in and out.
[*][b]Tags over heads[/b]: avatar, name, rank and job (DarkRP), health bar, and who is talking. Each player can turn them off.
[*][b]DarkRP HUD[/b]: health (with the damage you just took fading away), armor, hunger, money (showing what you just got or spent), salary, wanted/arrested/license, ammo, agenda and a lockdown banner. DarkRP messages show as Praetor notifications.
[*][b]DarkRP F4 menu[/b]: every job with a preview you can turn (and pick the model when the job has more than one), description, weapons, salary, slots and why you can't take it yet; become or ask for a vote. Shop with entities, weapons, shipments, ammo, food and vehicles, with search. Laws and a [b]bar of RP commands[/b] (drop and give money, sell doors, demote, advert, job name, gun license; police: wanted, warrant; mayor: lockdown, laws). It reads DarkRP's own tables, nothing to set up.
[*]The HUD, the F4 menu, the DarkRP TAB and the agenda each have their own switch in Settings > DarkRP.
[*][b]Loading screen[/b]: your server name, tips, map, slots and gamemode, your Discord, the real download progress with the file being downloaded, and music (M turns it off). English or Portuguese, following the game. Set it all in Settings > Loading screen. A server with its own loading screen keeps it until you turn this one on.
[*][b]Notifications, announcements, votes, reports and ban screen[/b] with the new design.
[*][b]Rank icon editor[/b] with two modes: [b]Pixel[/b] (16x16) and the new [b]Paint[/b] (32x32): brush and pencil in 6 sizes (mouse wheel), eraser, fill, eyedropper, mirror, undo and redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y).
[/list]

[h2]1.4.0[/h2]
[b]English[/b][list]
[*][b]Prop protection[/b]: everything a player spawns is theirs (props, entities, vehicles, NPCs, things made by tools and dupes). Players only touch their own things: physgun, gravgun, toolgun, context menu, unfreeze and damage. Nobody touches the things of others, not of a friend and not of the staff. Ranks with "Touch everyone's props" (moderator and up by default) hold, freeze, tool and delete anything, and deleting someone else's prop goes to the logs. Props of who left are removed after 5 minutes (they are theirs again if the player comes back first). Map entities are kept for the staff. A small card under the crosshair shows whose thing you aim at, and turns red and shakes when you try to touch something that is not yours. Speaks CPPI (wire, AdvDupe2...), and turns itself off when FPP (DarkRP) or another prop protection is installed.
[*][b]Ban screen[/b]: a banned player sees a full screen card with the reason, who banned, the date, the time left (live), the ban code with a copy button and [b]your Discord link[/b] (set in Settings > Bans and ban screen, with your own text). It shows when they are banned while playing and when they try to join again, then they are kicked. While on it they are invisible and frozen, can't talk or do anything. 3 tries in 10 minutes and only the Steam message shows, so a ban can't hold a slot. The Steam message also has the code and the link now.
[*][b]Ban lines in the chat[/b]: "X was banned by Y for Z. Reason: ..." for every ban (commands, ban tab, votes, linked accounts), and "X tried to join, but is banned (time left, reason, by)". Each one can be shown to everyone, only staff or nobody.
[*][b]Screenshot of a player's screen[/b]: !screengrab <player> or the "Screenshot" button on the player. The picture opens in a viewer (fit or real size), says who took it, when, the resolution and if the game was not the active window. The new "Screenshots" tab keeps the last 80 with search. If the game does not answer (a cheat can block it) it goes to the Security tab. Every screenshot goes to the logs.
[*][b]New report system[/b] (when Blackbox is not handling reports): the player picks who (the people they just fought or talked with come first, with "hurt you" or "talked in the chat"), what happened in category tiles, writes the details and can attach a picture of their own screen from the moment they opened the window. "My reports" shows the queue position and who is handling it, a small card on the side of the screen follows it live, the player talks with the staff inside the report, can cancel it, and says if it helped when it is closed.
[*][b]Report desk for the staff[/b]: a queue sorted by priority (cheating first, many reports against the same player go up, players with false reports count less), with the category, both players, how long it waits, who took it and "3 reports" when the same player is reported again. The report shows both players online or not, what the server saw (damage between them, their last chat lines, past reports and punishments, distance), the attached picture, the conversation with quick answers, and every action: sit, goto, bring, spectate, screenshot, sheet. Take, let go or take over, and close with an outcome (punished, warned, no proof, false report, solved) and a note the player reads. Reports nobody took ring again every 2 minutes.
[*][b]Linked accounts ban by default now[/b]: an account that joins from the address of a banned player is banned for the rest of that ban, with the reason "Tried to get around the ban of Name (STEAM_0:...)". The Family Sharing ban says the same with the SteamID of the banned account.
[*]Fix: the scheduled restart tab gave an error when the game opened.
[*]Fix: map pictures from the Workshop also load for players who join later.
[/list]

[*]Votes: [b]once you vote, the window closes and the vote is final[/b]. A small bar shows what you voted and the time left. The target of a vote doesn't vote on it. Staff can [b]cancel the current vote[/b] with the X on the vote itself or with !stopvote.
[*][b]Permission changes show in the chat[/b]: "X gave the rank Y the permission Z" (also when it is taken back or reset, and for a single player). Choose everyone, only staff or nobody in the settings.
[*][b]Dupe blocker remade[/b]: works with the Duplicator, AdvDupe2 and Adv Duplicator, counts props and constraints before pasting and warns the player and the staff with a card in every case (also when a superadmin goes over the limit). New option to make the limit count for everyone. The list of props blocked in dupes now starts empty.
[*][b]Anti-crash remade and on by default[/b]: watches the server tick and acts in steps (freezes stuck props, then the props of who is causing it, then everything moving, and only then removes what is still stuck). Freezes props that spawn inside each other, tunes the physics so collisions can't lock the server, catches broken physics (NaN and props flying off the map) and spawn spam, without blocking dupes being pasted.
[*][b]Security tab remade[/b]: server health card with a live tick chart and the anti-crash stage, who has the most moving props (go to, clear props, sheet), last actions, unfreeze all and a "clean the whole map" button. The event list has categories, search, severity colours, 24 hour chart, days and a menu on every event.
[*][b]Restriction badges remade[/b]: a round seal on the icon, items you can't use are dimmed with a lock, and a card follows the mouse with the picture, the rule and the rank needed. When the server refuses an item, the card shows right under the icon you clicked and shakes. The restriction tab shows a 3D preview of the model.
[*]Chat: "Nearby" and "Staff" tabs, the text box no longer freezes, microphone icon and send arrow fixed.
[*][b]Forms fixed everywhere[/b]: in announcements, reasons and other windows the boxes no longer jump to the bottom leaving only the labels, and the first box is ready to type.
[*]Player picker: shows the SteamID (STEAM_0:...) instead of the long ID, and the text no longer hides under the avatar frame.
